# DroidLinux
DroidLinux bring Linux to Android without emulation or chroot. FOR FREE

## How we did this?
We use the Standard Android Kernel and Magisk or TWRP to inject tools to your device that are configured to work with Android and is POSIX compatible.

FAQ:

Q: Does it brick Android in any way and/or are my apps safe?

A: Yes they are as DroidLinux just makes a folder that includes more folders that are in POSIX compatible order for some apps but apps that might have support for non POSIX devices will be installed outside to the folder

Q: Do i need root or do i need to unlock my bootloader?

A: You just need 1 since we distrubute DroidLinux in TWRP .zip files and Magisk Modules.
